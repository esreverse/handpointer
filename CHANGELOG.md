# Changelog

What changed in each version of Quite Handy. The section for a version is shown in the update dialog,
so write it for people using the app.



## 1.5.0

- Tiny Pointer is now called Hand Pointer
- Hand Pointer is a new app on your Mac: allow Accessibility once more in System Settings, and turn on Open at login again if you use it



## 1.4.0

- Undo and redo: Press U to take back the last line or number stamp, I to bring it back; hold to keep going
- Moving or removing a number stamp can be undone too
- The green "Allowed" in General is easier to read in light mode
- New installs start with the zoom loupe at 3×
- New app icon, and the name is now written as two words: Tiny Pointer
- New pointer: Christmas tree. The Cloud and Neon hands are gone



## 1.3.1

- Tiny Pointer is now signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it opens without the security warning
- After this update, allow Accessibility once more in System Settings, because the app's signature changed



## 1.3.0

- Arrows: Hold ⌃ while you draw and the line gets an open arrowhead pointing the way you drew, shown live
- Number stamps: Hold ⌥ and click to place numbered circles 1, 2, 3 … in your ink colour; drag one to move it, click it to remove it
- Smoothing: Lines are smoothed while you draw and still end right at the pointer, in four steps from off to medium; arrows always use the strongest
- New hold keys: ⇧ zoom, ⌃ arrows, ⌥ number stamps, ⌘ click your Mac for real
- Clicks and drags do nothing while you hold the zoom key
- The zoom loupe goes from 200 to 800 pt, and its Liquid Glass ring grows with it
- The skeleton hand no longer has a stray highlight
- New defaults for a fresh install: 600 pt loupe at 4×, sound on, light smoothing



## 1.2.0

- Color pointer: A dot in your stroke's size, colour and opacity instead of a hand, always last in the toolbar
- The toolbar, its tooltips and the zoom loupe use Liquid Glass on macOS 26 and later
- Tooltips and a hover state for everything in the toolbar
- Drawings stay below the toolbar
- The drag handle is easier to grab; right-click it to put the toolbar back
- Right-clicking the toolbar ends the session too, if that option is on
- Rainbow: The tap circle uses the colour where your last line ended, and with a 0° shift the next line carries on from it
- Next and previous pointer stop at the first and last one; hold the key to step through quickly
- ⌘, opens Settings while the pointer is on
- Automatic colour has its own "A" icon and comes first; the white glove draws in white
- New defaults for a fresh install



## 1.1.0

First release of Tiny Pointer, based on TinyHand 1.0.2 by Kamran Ahmed.

- Rainbow ink: Lines run through every colour, with adjustable speed and a hue shift for each new line
- Stroke size and line opacity can be set separately from the pointer size
- Tap circle: A circle grows and fades out where you tap
- Tap sound plays on tap only, or on tap and draw
- Zoom loupe: Hold a key to magnify the screen around the pointer
- Ink colours sit right in the toolbar
- The toolbar can be moved with the handle on its left; right-click the handle to put it back
- The toolbar can be shown or hidden, and gets out of the way while you interact with your Mac
- New shortcuts for clearing, colours, switching pointers, sound and the toolbar; single keys work while the pointer is on
- Optional: Right-click turns the pointer off
- Settings reorganised into General, Shortcuts, Draw, Zoom, Pointers, Sound and About
- Updates are checked and installed automatically
